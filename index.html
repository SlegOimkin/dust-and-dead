    01
    First Hunt

    Your first minute

    Press Start Hunt. You begin with a revolver, a clear objective, and no build to memorize.

    01

    Keep moving

    The dead win when they surround you. Circle open ground, leave yourself an escape lane, and never stare at one target for too long.

    02

    Collect what drops

    Walk over XP orbs to level up. Take ammo crates before your reserve is empty; each crate adds reserve ammo for every weapon you currently own.

    03

    Read, then choose

    Most levels offer three cards. Mobility and survival help when crowded; damage, ammo, and reload speed help when you already have room.

    V

    Every fifth wave is a boss hunt, starting at wave 5. Watch the warning text and boss HUD—each encounter has its own rhythm.

    04
    Between Hunts

    Where permanent progress lives

    Run cards disappear when the hunt ends. Dust, Contracts, unlocks, and your Wardrobe stay.

    Earned from play

    Dust

    Kills, cleared waves, and bosses bank Dust. Spend it in Unlocks on classes and new cards for future runs.

    Tracked automatically

    Contracts

    Long-term feats fill as you play. Each completed Contract adds one permanent Mark; Marks are milestones and are never spent.

    Open Unlocks from the main menu

    Classes
    Spend Dust to add new level 5 choices.
    Mastery
    See each default path and the Arsenal Contract that opens its second path.
    Cards
    Buy upgrades that can enter future drafts. Purchases affect the next hunt.
    Marked Deck
    After buying 10 cards, mark exactly 5 favorites. Eligible marked cards roll at twice the normal weight, never as a guarantee.
    Wardrobe

    Outfits and hats are cosmetic. They unlock from Contract Mark milestones or boss Contracts, and one is sold for Dust; simply apply the look you want.

    Local Multiplayer

    Fight zombies and rival cowboys in a nearby 2–4 player PvP match. Zombie and player kills earn points; the last survivor wins once they also hold the lead. Upgrade choices do not pause the fight.

    The best first habit: keep moving, collect your XP, and reload before the boss banner appears.
